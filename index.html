About

Agriculture grounded in practical experience.

Gert Lucas Roos, also known as Gerlu Roos, is a South African agribusiness and agricultural operations professional with experience spanning agricultural production, commodity trading, procurement, logistics, operational management and business development.

His professional interests centre on modern agriculture, efficient farm and production management, agricultural technology, commercial strategy and the movement of agricultural commodities from producer to market.

Gert has developed experience across both practical agricultural environments and commercial management, with an emphasis on building sustainable operations, strong supplier relationships and long-term business opportunities.
