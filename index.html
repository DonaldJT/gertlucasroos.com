About
Practical agriculture, commercial discipline and a long-term view of business.

Gert Lucas Roos is a South African agricultural professional whose experience spans practical farm environments and the commercial systems that support them. His work has included agricultural production, procurement, trading, logistics, administration, project coordination and customer and supplier development.

His professional focus is on making agricultural businesses work better as complete operating systems: people, machinery, production timing, finance, procurement, transport, technology and market access working together.

He has worked with agricultural and commercial activities in Southern Africa and has exposure to international agricultural projects and cross-border supply chains.
