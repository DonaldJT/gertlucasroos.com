Professional Profile

Gert Lucas Roos

Also known as Gerlu Roos, Gert Lucas Roos is a South African agribusiness and agricultural operations professional with interests spanning agricultural production, farm management, commodity trading, procurement, logistics and business development.

Agriculture & Agribusiness

Practical agriculture. Commercial thinking. Operational perspective.

Gert's professional interests combine practical agriculture with the commercial and operational disciplines required to build and manage resilient agricultural businesses.

His areas of interest include agricultural production, farm management, commodity markets, procurement, logistics, operational efficiency, agricultural technology and business development.

He maintains a particular interest in the future of African agriculture and in the systems, infrastructure and commercial thinking required to strengthen agricultural businesses and supply chains.
