Professional Experience

Working across the practical and commercial sides of agriculture.

Agriculture is ultimately an operational business. Strong results depend on practical execution, disciplined planning, sound commercial judgement and the ability to coordinate people, products, suppliers, customers and logistics effectively.

Gert's experience has developed across agricultural trading, operational management, procurement, logistics and project coordination. His work and professional interests are centred on connecting practical agriculture with the systems and commercial disciplines required to build resilient agricultural businesses.

Operational Perspective

Agriculture works best when the whole system works.

Production alone does not determine the success of an agricultural business. Procurement, logistics, people, machinery, finance, technology, market access and customer relationships all influence the final result.

Gert's professional approach is therefore based on viewing agriculture as an interconnected operating system rather than as a collection of isolated activities.

Working Principles

Practical principles that guide professional decision-making.

Practical execution matters. Plans only create value when they can be implemented effectively in real operating conditions.
Commercial discipline protects the operation. Cost control, procurement, logistics and market understanding are essential parts of sustainable agricultural performance.
Technology must improve decisions. Agricultural technology is most useful when it produces clearer information, better timing and measurable operational improvement.
Strong relationships build resilient businesses. Suppliers, customers, staff and commercial partners all form part of a successful agricultural value chain.
