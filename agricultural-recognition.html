Eastern Region dryland maize recognition.

Independent agricultural coverage of the 2018 PIONEER® Weigh and Win competition records Gerlu Roos and Heinrich Roos of Roos Boerdery, Wonderfontein, as winners of the dryland section in the Eastern Region.
